# AirTab - Mini Floating Browser (Chrome Extension Manifest V3)

A macOS Safari "Look Up" & Arc style in-page floating mini browser with Apple VisionOS ultra-translucent frosted glass, in-browser Full Window & PiP (Picture-in-Picture) video player, multi-tab manager, smart in-tab reload, adaptive smartphone mobile view, and sidebar docking.

---

## ⚡ Supercharged Features

### 1. ⚡ Instant Hover Look Up (Option / Alt Key)
- Hover your cursor over any word, term, or link on any webpage and press **`Option` (or `Alt`)**.
- AirTab instantly pops open with liquid spring blossoming, pointing its arrow directly at the hovered word or link!
- Press `Option` again to smoothly dismiss.
- Fully customizable hotkey in AirTab Settings (`Option / Alt`, `Shift`, `Control / Cmd`, `Spacebar`).

### 2. 📱 Adaptive Smartphone Mobile View (< 420px Width)
- Drag the bottom-right corner of AirTab narrow (< 420px) to automatically trigger **Mobile Mode**!
- Emulates an authentic **375px iPhone mobile phone layout** with zero horizontal overflow.
- Mobile header dynamically activates the `📱 Mobile` status pill, and non-essential desktop buttons collapse so the search omnibox has full breathing room.
- Drag wider (> 420px) to instantly return to full desktop floating card view!

### 3. 🗂️ Floating Side-Dock on Outside Click
- When AirTab is open and you click outside onto the main page, it smoothly morphs into a sleek frosted-glass edge tab (**`AirTab ✕`**) docked on the right side of the screen instead of vanishing!
- Single-click on the side pill instantly restores AirTab with all your tabs intact, with zero flicker.
- Click `✕` to dismiss completely.

### 4. 🪟 Apple VisionOS Translucent Frosted Glass
- **Ultra-Translucent Glass:** Dark & light frosted glass (`backdrop-filter: blur(48px) saturate(220%)`). Background content glows softly through the card.
- **📌 Persistent Pinning:** When you pin AirTab, it stays sticky and roams across tabs in the main browser.
- **◧ Dock to Sidebar (Edge/Arc Style):** Click `Dock` in the header or drag to snap AirTab into a full-height right sidebar with a live resizable handle.

### 5. 👆 macOS Trackpad Two-Finger Swipe Gesture Navigation
- Seamless two-finger swipe right to go **Back** and swipe left to go **Forward**, with visual Safari Look Up HUD chevron animation, active across all iframe frames.
- Keyboard shortcuts: `Cmd + [` / `Alt + Left` for Back, `Cmd + ]` / `Alt + Right` for Forward.

### 6. 📺 Picture-in-Picture (PiP) Mini Player & ⛶ Full Window View
- **📺 PiP Mode (`P`):** 1-click Picture-in-Picture floating mini-player docked at the bottom-right corner (`420px × 255px`). Draggable anywhere, stays on top while you browse.
- **⛶ Full Window View (`F`):** Expands AirTab smoothly to fill 98% of the browser window with zero OS monitor flashing.
- **🎬 Video Focus (YouTube):** Strips comments and sidebars, filling the entire card with the pure video player.
- **⤢ Theater Mode (`T`):** Widescreen floating card (`860px × 580px`).

### 7. 🔍 In-Tab Zoom Controls
- Direct **`100%` Zoom pill** in the Omnibox: Click to cycle zoom levels (`85%` ➔ `100%` ➔ `115%` ➔ `130%` ➔ `150%`).
- Keyboard shortcuts: `Cmd + Plus` / `Cmd + Minus` / `Cmd + 0` (Reset).

### 8. 🛡️ AirShield Offline AdBlocker
- Built-in DeclarativeNetRequest ruleset blocks intrusive ads, trackers, and video prerolls locally.

### 9. 🔒 100% Private & Offline-First
- Zero telemetry, zero tracking, zero remote scripts, and zero data collection. All browsing data stays 100% on your local machine.

## 🚀 How to Install & Reload in Google Chrome

1. Open Google Chrome and go to `chrome://extensions`.
2. Ensure **Developer mode** (top-right toggle) is enabled.
3. Click the **🔄 Refresh** button on the **AirTab** card.
4. Open any webpage and refresh it (`Cmd+R` or `F5`).
5. Select any text to see the floating `AirTab` pill, hover over a word/link and tap `Option`, or press `Alt+S`!
